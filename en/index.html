Creative spaces

Three houses that stood empty, now back with the town

Empty rooms in old buildings are brought back through activity and design, so the community can earn, visitors can feel the town, and the identity, way of life, culture and character of the place stay intact.

78Kuapa Mining Museum
กั่วป่าเหมืองมิวเซียม

House of the mine owner

This was once the home of a mine owner and a tin-mining company. The space is now Kuapa Mining Museum (กั่วป่าเหมืองมิวเซียม), with mining tools, survey maps and documents from the mines.

Programmes in the space rotate each month.

109Kang Tang Yok Guan
ศาลเจ้ากังตั้งโหยกวน (หวุ่ยกุ่น)

A Chinese association and shrine

This is the association of Hakka Chinese (จีนฮากก้า · จีนแคะ). The second floor still holds Tham Kung Ya (พระท่ามกุงหย่า), a figure Chinese devotees revere. The ground floor stood unused, so the group asked the shrine keepers for leave to hold exhibitions there.

Kuapapoh Gallery · exhibitions and creative activity on the local wisdom of Takua Pa people.

305Tao Ming School
โรงเรียนเต้าหมิง

A Chinese school from the mining era

Tao Ming School (โรงเรียนเต้าหมิง) once flourished in the tin-mining years. The team wants to bring it back as a creative space and a place of memory, through “Tao Ming, a spark of learning” (เต้าหมิง ประกายแสงแห่งการเรียนรู้). The building now holds knowledge of the town: architecture, culture, rites and other stories of Takua Pa.

Tao Ming emblem (ตราสัญลักษณ์เต้าหมิง)

The team is also turning other empty rooms into supporting venues, so the whole town can be a creative town, in the spirit of Kuapapoh, show the town (กั่วป่าโพ้ จีโชว์เมือง).

Where the name comes from

What “Kuapapoh” means

Kuapapoh (กั่วป่าโพ้) is the Hokkien Chinese name for Takua Pa. It comes from Chinese who settled here in the tin-mining era.

กั่วป่า Kua Pa · Takua Pa, the old town
โพ้ (埠) Poh · market or port, the landing that gave rise to the town

Together the name means Takua Pa port market (ตลาดท่าเรือตะกั่วป่า). Chinese traders and tin miners once arrived by junk at the Takua Pa landing. In another sense it also means the people of Takua Pa (คนตะกั่วป่า). During the Vegetarian Festival the word still lives in shrine rites, and became a sacred name for the town.

The 6 O heritage

Showing the town through six heritage themes

Attireอาภรณ์

Local dress, lace, batik sarongs, textile patterns and jewellery.

Livingอาศัย

Everyday life, shops, how people live, and rites that sit easily with daily life.

Foodอาหาร

Local food, inherited recipes, and eateries from the mining era that are still open.

Moodอารมณ์

Artists of Kuapa, whether painting, song and dance, or drums in the Vegetarian Festival.

Shrinesอาราม

Temples, shrines and yok guan (โหย่กวน) throughout the old town, and places to rest.

Buildingsอาคาร

Houses in the district, architecture that holds attitude and belief, heritage worth keeping.

Meet Chang Pong

About the Artist

Tinnapop Ngansathinติณณภพ งานสถิร

He was born in Takua Pa old market. He is an alumnus of Sutthitham Anusorn School (โรงเรียนสุทธิธรรมอนุสรณ์) and Takua Pa “Senanukul” School (โรงเรียนตะกั่วป่า “เสนานุกูล”). Art held him from childhood. He drew cartoons, superheroes, basketball players and Chinese deities absorbed from the culture around Takua Pa people.

He studied at the College of Fine Arts (วิทยาลัยช่างศิลป), then took a degree in painting at the Faculty of Painting, Sculpture and Graphic Arts, Silpakorn University. After graduating he has taught art to young people and to many organisations.

He has shown in major exhibitions, including “After the Missing Conversation” (หลังจากบทสนทนาที่หายไป) at Sanam Chandra Art Gallery, Silpakorn University · “The Wonder of Conservation Orchids” (มหัศจรรย์กล้วยไม้แห่งรักษ์) at Siam Paragon · and the 6th, 7th and 8th exhibitions of the International Association of Art in Thailand.

He works as a professional illustrator of buildings and places in Phang Nga and nearby provinces. He drew scenes for the Hollywood film RAMBO 6, and book covers for well-known publishers. He is now assistant art officer at Takua Pa Town Municipality, Phang Nga, and still works as an independent artist.

“The Peranakan Charm paintings are a record of who I have been at every stage of the path. They shaped a person who loves art, living inside Peranakan culture.”Artist Statement · Still Becoming

🌱 Who is Jampoon

Jampoon takes after the champoon flower (ดอกจำปูน), the flower of Phang Nga province named in the provincial motto. The champoon is a native plant of the South with firm 3-petalled blooms, strongly fragrant in the daytime.

💛 Why green and yellow

The young bloom is green and the full bloom turns yellow, with scent drifting out to people who pass by. The designer keeps both stages in one character, a story of beginning and growth. So Jampoon wears a green petal head and a yellow petal body.

🎨 From a design contest

Jampoon was drawn for the PHANGNGA MASCOT DESIGN CONTEST, held by the Phang Nga Tourism Business Association with the Phang Nga Provincial Administration, Phang Nga province and the TCAP Association. The brief asked for the province's public relations and happiness officer, and Jampoon's mission is to go out and bring everyone fun and friendship. Jampoon won 3rd place in this contest.

FAQ

Questions visitors ask

What is Kuapapoh?

Kuapapoh (กั่วป่าโพ้) is a creative cultural district in Takua Pa Old Town, Phang Nga. Empty rooms in three historic houses, 78, 109 and 305, are used for exhibitions, design and community activity, while the identity, way of life and culture of the place stay in view.

Where is Takua Pa Old Town?

Takua Pa Old Town is in Phang Nga province, Thailand. House 78 is 78 Studio Takuapa on Sri Takua Pa Road (ถนนศรีตะกั่วป่า). You can open the old town in Google Maps from this page.

When are the three houses open?

House 78 is open every day. House 109 is open on Sundays only. House 305 is open every Sunday.

What does the name Kuapapoh mean?

Kuapapoh is the Hokkien Chinese name for Takua Pa. Kua Pa (กั่วป่า) means Takua Pa, and Poh (โพ้, 埠) means market or port, so the name means Takua Pa port market. It can also mean the people of Takua Pa.

What exhibition is on now?

Peranakan Charm · Still Becoming (มนต์เสน่ห์เปอรานากัน), a solo exhibition by Chang Pong · Tinnapop Ngansathin (ช่างป๋อง · ติณณภพ งานสถิร). It is on in September 2026 at 78 Studio on Takua Pa Cultural Road (ถนนวัฒนธรรมตะกั่วป่า).

What are the six heritage themes?

The district tells the town through six Thai words that start with อ., known as the 6 O: Attire (อาภรณ์), Living (อาศัย), Food (อาหาร), Mood (อารมณ์), Shrines (อาราม) and Buildings (อาคาร). The first theme on show is Attire.

Who supports Kuapapoh?

Kuapapoh sits under the Creative Cultural District (CCD) programme. It is supported and run by the Creative Economy Agency (CEA), working with King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL).
